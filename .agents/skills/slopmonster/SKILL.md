# SlopMonster — Anti-AI Slop & Humanizer Skill

Detects and strips out formulaic "AI slop" from text, making content sound like it was crafted by an authentic, discerning human.

## The 5 AI Tells to Eliminate:
1. **Fluff Vocabulary:** "Delve", "tapestry", "unleash", "beacon", "game-changer", "supercharge", "testament", "nestled".
2. **Predictable Structures:** "Not only... but also", "Whether you are X or Y...", "In today's fast-paced digital world...".
3. **The Rule of Three:** Artificial triplets forced into every sentence ("flexible, reliable, and scalable").
4. **Vague Proof:** Unsubstantiated claims ("proven track record", "trusted by industry leaders").
5. **Over-explanation:** Explaining obvious concepts or summarizing concluding thoughts ("In conclusion, ...").

## Execution Workflow
- Strip marketing jargon and replace with plain, punchy facts.
- Use tight, asymmetric sentences with natural cadence.
- State what the product or code does directly, without preambles.
